Cosine Similarity: Direction Over Magnitude

The dot product mixes two things: how aligned two vectors are, and how long they are. For search we only care about the first one.

1. The problem with the raw dot product

Two documents can mean the same thing, but if one embedding is simply longer, the dot product scores it higher. Length is noise here. Cosine similarity divides the length out:

cos(A, B) = (A · B) / (‖A‖ × ‖B‖)

The result is always between −1 and 1: 1 = same direction, 0 = unrelated (perpendicular), −1 = opposite.

3. Why this matters for your search engine

  • Your query embedding and each movie embedding are compared pairwise, then ranked by score.
  • Ranking by cosine means a long, wordy description is not favoured just for being long.
