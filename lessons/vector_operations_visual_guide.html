The 3 Operations You Need

You don't need a degree in pure mathematics—search relies on three intuitive building blocks.

Operation 01

The Dot Product

Multiplies corresponding coordinates along every dimension and sums them up. Measures coordinate-level agreement.

$$\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = \sum_{i=1}^{d} u_i v_i = u_1 v_1 + u_2 v_2 + \dots + u_d v_d$$
Intuition: If both vectors have large numbers in the "superhero" dimensions, the sum grows large and positive.
Operation 02

Vector Magnitude (\(L_2\) Norm)

The straight-line Euclidean distance from the origin \((0,0)\) to the vector tip. Quantifies vector length.

$$\|\vec{v}\| = \sqrt{v_1^2 + v_2^2 + \dots + v_d^2}$$
Why it matters: Longer text shouldn't get higher scores just because it has more words. We normalize length to 1.
Operation 03

Cosine Similarity

Divides the dot product by both vector lengths to compute strictly the angle \(\theta\), eliminating document length bias.

$$\cos(\theta) = \frac{\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v}}{\|\vec{u}\| \|\vec{v}\|}$$
The Pro Trick: When vectors are pre-normalized to length 1, \(\|\vec{u}\|\|\vec{v}\| = 1\), so Cosine Similarity is simply the Dot Product!
